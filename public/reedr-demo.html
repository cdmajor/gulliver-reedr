OpenAI Releases GPT-5, Its Most Capable Reasoning Model Yet

By Kyle Wiggers  ·  July 25, 2025  ·  4 min read

OpenAI on Friday released GPT-5, a new flagship model the company claims represents a significant leap in reasoning ability over its predecessors. The model, available via the API and ChatGPT, can solve complex math, science, and coding problems with a degree of accuracy that OpenAI says approaches expert-level performance.

GPT-5 introduces a new "deep thinking" mode that allocates additional compute at inference time, allowing it to deliberate before responding. In internal benchmarks, GPT-5 scored 87% on the AIME 2025 math competition and surpassed PhD-level accuracy on GPQA Diamond, a difficult graduate-level science test.

"We believe GPT-5 is the most capable model we have ever released," Sam Altman, OpenAI's CEO, wrote in a blog post Friday. "It represents a new class of intelligence that can reason through hard problems the way a domain expert would."
